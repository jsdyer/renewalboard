Your domains

Renewal board

The calendar holds an all-day event on each expiry date, with reminders 30 and 7 days before. Import it into Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. It's a snapshot, so download it again after you renew.

What leaves your browser. Each domain is sent from your browser to IANA (to find its registry), to that registry's RDAP service (CIRA for .ca), and, if certificates are on, to crt.sh. Nothing is sent to coolcode.ca. Your list is kept only if you tick "Remember this list", which saves it in this browser under coolcode:renewalboard:list. Unticking removes it. Registries publish expiry dates differently, and some (including several country-code domains) publish none.
